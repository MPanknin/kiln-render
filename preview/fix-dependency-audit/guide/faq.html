FAQ ​

Which browsers are supported? ​

Any with WebGPU and hardware acceleration enabled. As of late 2026 (current status):

  • Chrome / Edge 113+ on Windows, macOS and ChromeOS; 121+ on Android. On Linux, 144+ for Intel Gen12+ GPUs and 147+ for NVIDIA on Wayland; other setups need --enable-unsafe-webgpu.
  • Safari 26+ on macOS, iOS, iPadOS and visionOS.
  • Firefox 141+ on Windows; on Apple-silicon Macs 145+ (macOS 26) or 147+. Linux and Intel Macs: Nightly only.

Without WebGPU, KilnViewer.create() rejects with an error, which the demo shows on the page.

Can Kiln read my data? ​

  • OME-Zarr (NGFF v0.4/v0.5) loads directly from a URL or local directory: uint8, uint16, int16, float32 (float64 is read as float32). See OME-Zarr.
  • Raw volumes are converted once to the sharded binary format with the bundled CLI.

Why won't my dataset load from S3 or my own server? ​

Almost always CORS. The server must send Access-Control-Allow-Origin for your site, and allow the Range header for sharded binary and sharded Zarr. See Hosting.

How much VRAM does Kiln use? ​

The atlas and indirection table stay within a fixed budget, ~1.3 GiB by default (atlasBudgetBytes), whatever the dataset size. Render targets come on top. See Memory budget.

What are the known rendering issues? ​

See Known issues.

Can I use Kiln in my own application? ​

Yes, it's on npm under Apache 2.0. Use KilnViewer (Quick start), or KilnEngine if your app already has a WebGPU device and render loop (Bring your own device).
