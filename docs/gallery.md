## Dataset credits

- **Chameleon** — CT scan of *Chamaeleo calyptratus*. Digital Morphology, 2003.
- **Beechnut** — MicroCT scan. Computer-Assisted Paleoanthropology group, University of Zurich.
- **Pancreas** — Abdominal CT (int16), from the Open SciVis Datasets collection.
- **Stag Beetle** — Industrial CT scan. Meister Eduard Gröller, Georg Glaeser, Johannes Kastner, 2005.
- **Woodbranch, Backpack, Kingsnake, Pawpawsaurus** — CT and micro-CT scans from the [Open SciVis Datasets](https://github.com/sci-visus/open-scivis-datasets) collection.
- **Vibrio cholerae** — Cryo-ET tomogram. [CryoET Data Portal](https://cryoetdataportal.czscience.com/).
- **IDR0079** — Zebrafish lateral line cellular architecture. [Hartmann et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.55913). [Image Data Resource](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/).
- **IDR0047** — Yeast smFISH mRNA expression. [Li & Neuert, 2019](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-019-0106-6). [Image Data Resource](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/).
- **S-BSST410** — Zebrafish embryo, axis specification. [BioImage Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/studies/S-BSST410).
- **S-BIAD425** — *Arabidopsis* FLC gene expression (smFISH). [BioImage Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/biostudies/studies/S-BIAD425).
- **Fly-eFISH** — *Drosophila* brain, multiplexed expansion-assisted FISH. [Janelia Fly-eFISH Project Team](https://www.janelia.org/project-team/fly-efish).

Single-channel datasets from the [Open SciVis Datasets](https://github.com/sci-visus/open-scivis-datasets)
collection and the [CryoET Data Portal](https://cryoetdataportal.czscience.com/).
Multichannel datasets from the [Image Data Resource](https://idr.openmicroscopy.org/), the
[EBI BioImage Archive](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/bioimage-archive/) and Janelia's Fly-eFISH project.
